# WideAngle suite

- **Photos:** image library, organization, culling, and photo editing.
- **Motion:** standalone video and lyrical creation, with its own media and projects.
- Shared visual family and app-switch links; no shared database or required process.

## Shipped foundation

Studio dashboard, direct multi-file import, media previews, named independent
projects, autosave, duplicate/rename/delete, single-clip editing, lyrical videos,
queued export/cancel, export history, downloads, and responsive layouts.

Lyric-to-image backgrounds: editable scene prompts, shared style/creative direction,
OpenAI-compatible image generation, locally stored server credentials, persistent
scene results, cancellation and retry of failed scenes, and timed slideshow insertion.
